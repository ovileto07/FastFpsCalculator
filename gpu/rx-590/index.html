What can change FPS with RX 590

The same GPU can produce very different frame rates depending on the game and settings. 1080p, 1440p and 4K place different demands on the graphics pipeline, while quality presets, ray tracing and upscaling can move performance substantially.

When GPU performance matters most

GPU-heavy games and higher resolutions usually make graphics-card differences more visible. If the CPU becomes the limit, upgrading the GPU may produce a smaller gain than expected.

How to read the estimate
