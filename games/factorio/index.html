What affects FPS?

Frame rate depends on the GPU, CPU, resolution and graphics settings. Upscaling and ray tracing can also change the workload. The same PC can produce different FPS in different areas of a game because scenes and effects are not equally demanding.

Why estimates vary

FastFPSCalculator provides a model-based estimate rather than a guaranteed benchmark. Drivers, game updates, background processes, temperatures, memory configuration and the exact scene can all affect real performance.

How to use the Factorio FPS page

Start with your actual CPU and GPU, then choose the resolution and quality preset you normally use. Compare the result at 1080p, 1440p or 4K when deciding whether an upgrade is worthwhile. If your target is a high-refresh display, pay particular attention to whether the CPU or GPU is the limiting component.

For competitive settings

Lower visual settings can reduce GPU workload, but CPU limits may become more important when you are targeting very high frame rates.

For high visual quality

Higher resolutions, demanding presets and ray tracing generally increase GPU workload. Upscaling can change the balance and may raise the estimated FPS.
