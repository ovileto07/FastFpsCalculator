Use FastFPSCalculator as a quick PC performance checker. Select the CPU and GPU in your system, choose a game and resolution, and see an estimated gaming FPS range.

Can I use it for my current PC?

Yes. Select the processor and graphics card closest to your system, then choose the game and settings you normally use. The estimate is intended to help you understand the expected performance of that configuration.

PC gaming at 1080p, 1440p and 4K

Resolution changes the workload substantially. 1080p can expose CPU limits on fast GPUs, while 1440p and 4K generally place more pressure on the graphics card. Always compare at the resolution you actually plan to play.

Planning a PC upgrade

Run your current CPU and GPU first, then compare a potential replacement. This makes it easier to see whether a GPU upgrade, CPU upgrade or a more balanced combination is likely to make the biggest difference.
