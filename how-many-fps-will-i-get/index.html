What affects how many FPS you get?

The biggest factors are the game, graphics card, processor, resolution and graphics quality. RAM, upscaling, ray tracing, drivers, temperatures and background processes can also change the final result.

Is the result exact?

No. The result is an estimate designed for planning and comparison. Real gameplay can move above or below the estimate depending on the map, scene, game update and system configuration.

How to use the result

Compare the estimated range with your target. Around 60 FPS is a common goal for general gaming, while 120–144 FPS or higher can be useful for competitive games and high-refresh displays.
