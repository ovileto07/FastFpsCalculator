About FastFPSCalculator

What is FastFPSCalculator?

FastFPSCalculator is a browser-based tool designed to help PC gamers estimate expected FPS from a selected CPU, GPU, game, resolution and graphics settings. The site also provides hardware comparisons and PC upgrade information.

Our goal

The goal is to make PC performance research quick and easy. Instead of requiring users to search through many benchmark pages, FastFPSCalculator presents an estimate in a simple calculator interface.

How the estimates work

The calculator uses the hardware and game data available on the site and applies a model to produce an estimated FPS result. Estimates are intended as a useful guide, not as a guarantee of the exact FPS a particular computer will achieve.

About the creator

FastFPSCalculator was created by Ovidiu Eduard Cimpoaca.

Important note

Actual gaming performance can vary depending on drivers, RAM configuration, background applications, game updates, graphics settings and many other factors. For that reason, users should treat calculator results as estimates.
